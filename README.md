# WeSendTestMail

**WeSendTestMail** is a free, simple, and lightweight email testing tool that sends a real test email directly to your inbox. Instantly verify if your email address is receiving messages correctly.

🔗 **Live Demo:** [https://wesendtestmail.com](https://wesendtestmail.com)

---

## ✨ Features

- 📧 **Send real test emails** — Directly to your inbox
- 🔓 **100% Free** — No signup, no credit card, no hidden charges
- 🚫 **No ads** — Clean, distraction-free experience
- ⚡ **Instant delivery** — Usually within 5-10 seconds
- 🌐 **Works with any email provider** — Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo, ProtonMail, custom domains
- 🔒 **Privacy focused** — We don't store or share your email address
- 📱 **Mobile responsive** — Works perfectly on all devices

---

## 🚀 How It Works

1. Enter your email address
2. Click "Send Test Email"
3. Check your inbox (or spam folder)

That's it! No technical knowledge required.
